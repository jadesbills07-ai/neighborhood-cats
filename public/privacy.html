Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This page describes, in plain language, what “Where in the Whiskers?” collects and what it does — and does not — show to other people.

← Back to the app

What this app is

“Where in the Whiskers?” is a shared Maine app where cat owners in a town can list their cats, see whether a cat is Outdoor OK or Indoor Only, report sightings, and message a cat’s owner. You only see cats for your own town — you cannot browse other towns.

Sign-in: Google only, no passwords stored by this app

Sign-in is by Google sign-in only. Google handles your password — this app never asks for it and never stores a password for you. When you sign in with Google, the app requests only the standard openid email profile information. The app stores your Google user ID (never shown to other users), a display name, and the ZIP / county / town you chose. Your email address and Google ID are not shown to other users.

You can sign out at any time from the app. Signing out on one device does not delete your account or your cats; sign back in with the same Google account, on any phone or browser, to pick up where you left off.

Guests can browse a town read-only without signing in. Adding cats, messaging, reporting sightings, and using Lost / Emergency Mode require a signed-in Google account.

What you provide, and what other people see

  • Display name, ZIP, county, and town: used to place you in your town and to show you only that town’s cats. Other users in your town see your display name, not your email or Google ID.
  • Cats you add: name, description, collar/ID, home area, status (Outdoor OK / Indoor Only), photo if you upload one, and messages and sightings about that cat. These are shared with other users in your town.
  • Photos: photos you upload are resized in your browser and stored with the cat, so neighbours in your town can see them.
  • Messages and sightings: messages in a cat’s thread and sighting reports are shared with users in that town.

Location: approximate home pins, exact sighting pins only

Home pins are approximate by design. When you add a cat, give a street or nearest corner only — never a house number. The app rejects a home area that starts with a house number. Your home pin is approximate, not your exact address.

GPS at join: when you join or set up your account, you may tap “Confirm my location.” That GPS check only confirms you are in Maine and marks your account as GPS-confirmed. Your GPS coordinates are not stored as your home location — only your ZIP / county / town and a GPS-confirmed flag are stored. There is no ZIP-boundary check, so GPS never rejects a neighbour at a town edge for a mismatch.

GPS for sightings: if you report a sighting and choose “Use my current location,” that moment’s exact location is stored with the sighting and shown as a pin, so the owner knows exactly where the cat was seen. That exact pin is only for sightings — it is not your home address, and it is the spotter’s location at that moment, not the cat’s home.

Lost / Emergency Mode and exact addresses

Exact address, contact / phone, vet, and medical details are only shown in two cases:

  • When the cat’s owner puts that cat into Lost / Emergency Mode, the details the owner filled in (and only those fields) are shown to users in that town, with the cat highlighted, until the owner turns Emergency Mode off again; or
  • When the owner ticks “Always show my exact address” for a cat — that one field, the address only, is then shown normally. Contact, vet, and medical details still stay hidden until Emergency Mode.

Otherwise, exact address and contact details are never shown. Emergency details are stored on the server, but are not returned to other users in normal mode. Owner PINs, where set, are stored only as a hash and are never shown or returned.

Please note: once details are shown in Emergency Mode, people who saw them while it was on cannot be made to unsee them. Only turn Emergency Mode on when you want those details visible to your town.

Where your information is stored

Your account, cats, photos, messages, and sightings are stored in the app’s database on its hosting server (Render). Guest profile details you type without signing in are kept only in that browser on that device, not on the server, and are not shown publicly. The app is hosted on a free tier, so data is best treated as a community trial record, not a permanent archive — keep your own copies of anything important.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not show your email address, Google ID, or password (we do not hold one) to other users.
  • We do not let users browse towns other than their own.
  • We do not use your GPS coordinates as a home address, except for the exact sighting pin you choose to create.

Removing your information

There is not yet a self-serve delete button in the app — that is a known limitation, and this policy does not claim otherwise. To ask for your account and your cats to be removed, contact the app owner through a message in the app, and the owner can remove them from the app’s database. Signing out alone does not delete your account or your cats.

Questions about this policy

Questions about this policy go to the app owner through a message in the app. If this policy changes in a way that affects what is shown to other users, the updated version will be posted on this page.
